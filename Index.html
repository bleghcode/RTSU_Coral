How to use:
• Paste a note (one or many groups — separate groups with a line ---) and click Analyze Note & Add. Quantities, activity types, boat/lunch cost and the date are read from the note.
• Manual entry: click Add Empty Row and type in the table. Profit is always calculated = Sales − Net Cost.
• Your data is saved automatically in this browser. Use Backup / Restore to keep a copy or move to another computer.
• External report: use the preset below to hide cost/profit columns before printing for clients.
